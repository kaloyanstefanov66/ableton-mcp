# Alternative metal / nu-metal (Deftones, Linkin Park, Korn, Incubus era)

**Tempo / feel:** 75–110 BPM, often felt in half-time; hip-hop–influenced kick syncopation.
Grooves sit back. Chugs are drop-tuned and the kick locks to them in heavy parts.
**Timekeeper:** 8th or 16th hats in verses; crash or china quarters in choruses; ride bell for tension.
**Kit/sound:** big punchy kick, cracking snare, trashy china. Acuff Kit.

## Grooves
### Verse – hip-hop-leaning
```
H  x.o.|x.o.|x.o.|x.o.
S  ....|X..g|..g.|X...
K  x..x|....|..x.|.x..
```
### Pre-chorus – tension
```
B  x...|x...|x...|x...      ride bell quarters
S  ....|X...|....|X...
K  x.x.|....|x.xx|....
```
### Chorus – full and aggressive
```
C  X...|x...|X...|x...      crash every beat, china on the 1 of each phrase
N  x...|....|....|....
S  ....|X...|....|X...
K  x.xx|..x.|x.xx|..x.      follow the guitar chugs
```
### Heavy half-time (bridge / breakdown)
```
N  x...|x...|x...|x...
S  ....|....|X...|....
K  x.xx|x..x|..xx|x.x.      doubles the riff
```

## Fills
- 16th snare-to-tom descents over beat 4. End with a crash + kick on the 1.
- Stop-time: everything drops for an 8th before the chorus downbeat.
- Short flams on the snare into section changes.

## Dynamics
Snare backbeat 115–127, ghost notes 25–40, hats 60–85 with an accent on the beat.
Choruses are loud across the board; verses leave room for vocals.

## Signature moves
- The kick mirrors the guitar's syncopated chug while the snare stays on 2 and 4 (or on 3 in half-time).
- Open hat on the "and" of 4 leading into the next bar.
- Verse → chorus contrast through hats → crash-ride, not just more notes.

## Avoid
Constant double-kick, thrash-speed beats, ride-only choruses.
