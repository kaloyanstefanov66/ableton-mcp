# Modern / progressive metal and djent (Meshuggah, Periphery, Tesseract, Gojira, Spiritbox)

**Tempo / feel:** 90–160 BPM, machine-tight. Riffs often run in odd groupings over a steady 4/4
pulse, and the kick follows the riff's grouping while snare and cymbals keep the 4/4.
**Timekeeper:** china or crash quarters, hats/ride bell quarters; ghost notes in clean sections.
**Kit:** Acuff Kit. Keep velocities consistent (little humanizing).

## Grooves
### Polymetric djent groove
The riff repeats in, e.g., 7 or 9 sixteenths while the snare stays on 3 (half-time) and the
cymbal on quarters.
```
B  x...|x...|x...|x...
S  ....|....|X...|....
K  xx.x|x.x.|.xx.|x.xx      write the kick from the riff's chugs, not from the bar
```
### Half-time heavy
```
N  x...|x...|x...|x...
S  ....|....|X...|....
K  x.xx|..x.|x.xx|.x..
```
### Clean/ambient section – ghosty
```
R  o.o.|o.o.|o.o.|o.o.
S  ..g.|X.g.|.g..|X..g
K  x...|...x|..x.|....
```

## Fills
Displaced 16th/32nd fills that end on an unexpected 16th; tom runs grouped in 3s or 5s that resolve on the 1.

## Dynamics
Consistent and loud in heavy parts (kick 115, snare 122, china 112). Ghost notes 25–35 in clean sections.

## Signature moves
Kick locked to an odd-grouped riff while the snare anchors the 4/4. China on every quarter.
Sudden drops to just a ride bell.
