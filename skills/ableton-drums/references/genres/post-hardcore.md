# Post-hardcore / emo / screamo (Thursday, Saosin, Glassjaw, At the Drive-In, Underoath)

**Tempo / feel:** 130–190 BPM. Restless and dynamic, with frequent feel changes inside one song:
fast punk beats, disco hats, half-time breakdowns, odd accents with the guitar.
**Timekeeper:** fast 8th/16th hats; open-hat "disco" offbeats in choruses; crash-ride walls.
**Kit:** Dry Session, Corvaire, Acuff for heavier bands.

## Grooves
### Verse – busy 16th hats
```
H  xoxo|xoxo|xoxo|xoxo
S  ....|X..g|.g..|X...
K  x..x|..x.|.x..|....
```
### Chorus – disco-punk
```
O  ..x.|..x.|..x.|..x.      open hat on every offbeat
H  x...|x...|x...|x...
S  ....|X...|....|X...
K  x...|x...|x...|x...
```
### Punk/skank drive (fast sections)
```
C  x.x.|x.x.|x.x.|x.x.
S  ..x.|..x.|..x.|..x.
K  x...|x...|x...|x...
```
### Breakdown – half-time
```
N  x...|x...|x...|x...
S  ....|....|X...|....
K  x..x|..x.|x..x|..x.
```

## Fills
Fast 16th snare/tom flurries, linear fills (no two limbs at once), unison accents with the guitar
followed by a sudden stop. Floor-tom 8ths build into the last chorus.

## Dynamics
Wide range: whispery verses (hats 50–70) against explosive choruses (110–127). Lots of ghost notes.

## Signature moves
- Switch feel every section: verse busy, chorus disco, bridge half-time.
- Hit accents with the guitarist rather than keeping time through them.
- Big crash-ride wash under screamed parts.

## Avoid
One groove for the whole song; metal double-kick everywhere.
