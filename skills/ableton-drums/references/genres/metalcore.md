# Metalcore (Killswitch Engage, As I Lay Dying, Architects, Parkway Drive, Bring Me the Horizon)

**Tempo / feel:** 120–200 BPM. Verses alternate blasts/skank and double-kick; choruses open up
into half-time or straight backbeats; breakdowns are slow and kick-locked.
**Timekeeper:** china and crash dominate; open hats in melodic choruses; ride bell in builds.
**Kit:** Acuff Kit.

## Grooves
### Verse – double-kick drive
```
C  x...|x...|x...|x...
S  ....|X...|....|X...
K  xxxx|xxxx|xxxx|xxxx
```
### Melodic chorus – half-time with open hats
```
O  x.x.|x.x.|x.x.|x.x.
S  ....|....|X...|....
K  x...|..x.|x.x.|....
```
### Breakdown – kick follows the chugs
```
N  x...|x...|x...|x...
S  ....|....|X...|....
K  xx..|x..x|.x..|x...      copy the guitar exactly, leave the gaps empty
```
### Blast (short bursts only)
```
C  x.x.|x.x.|x.x.|x.x.
S  .x.x|.x.x|.x.x|.x.x
K  x.x.|x.x.|x.x.|x.x.
```

## Fills
16th snare builds into breakdowns, tom-and-kick triplets, and "the drop": a full bar of silence,
or just a china, before the breakdown hits.

## Dynamics
Kicks 110–125, snare 120+, chinas 110+. Choruses slightly lighter on cymbals.

## Signature moves
Breakdowns where every guitar chug is a kick and the snare lands on beat 3 of a half-time bar;
china quarters; a pre-breakdown stop.
