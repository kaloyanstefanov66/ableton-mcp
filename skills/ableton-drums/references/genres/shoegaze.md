# Shoegaze

**Tempo / feel:** two modes. Dreamy and slow (65–100 BPM, often half-time feel) or a motorik
drive (120–160 BPM) under a wall of guitar. Drums sit *inside* the wash, not on top of it.
**Timekeeper:** ride (bow, washy) or loose 8th hats; crash-ride for the loud parts.
**Kit/sound:** dry kit plus lots of reverb, soft kick (felt), toms ring. Corvaire, Plymouth, Dry Session.

## Grooves
### Verse – dreamy half-time
```
R  o.o.|o.o.|o.o.|o.o.
S  ....|....|X...|....
K  x...|....|....|..o.
F  ....|..g.|....|....
```
### Verse – motorik drive
```
H  x.x.|x.x.|x.x.|x.x.
S  ....|x...|....|x...
K  x...|x...|x...|x...
```
### Chorus / wall of sound
```
C  x...|x...|x...|x...      crash-ride on quarters (alternate two crashes if the kit has them)
S  ....|X...|....|X...
K  x...|..x.|x...|..x.
```
### Tribal tom build (pre-chorus)
```
F  x.x.|x.x.|x.x.|x.x.
T3 ....|..o.|....|..o.
S  ....|X...|....|X...
K  x...|....|x...|....
```

## Fills
- Slow tom rolls down the kit across a full beat or two, with 8ths rather than 16ths.
- Cymbal swells: crash 8ths crescendo (velocity 50 → 110) over the last bar.
- Space works as a fill too: drop everything except the ride for a bar.

## Dynamics
Verses are soft and even (velocity 70–95). Choruses are loud but not spiky (100–118). Ghost
notes are rare; the wash comes from cymbals, not snare chatter.

## Signature moves
- Ride and crash wash held for whole sections.
- Floor-tom heartbeat instead of a kick pattern.
- Long build sections that just repeat with rising cymbal intensity.

## Avoid
Busy 16th hats, double-kick, tight quantized metal feel, frequent fills.
