# Colm Ó Cíosóig (My Bloody Valentine)

Read with `genres/shoegaze.md`.

**Feel:** propulsive but buried. Simple, relentless patterns that turn into texture under the
guitar wash. Occasional frantic tom work. The kit sounds compressed and roomy.
**Tempo range:** 90–170 BPM; both dreamy mid-tempo and fast driving songs.

## Vocabulary
- Straight 8th or 16th hats and ride, played evenly with little accenting.
- Tom-heavy driving patterns: floor tom on every 8th instead of hats.
- Snare backbeat often doubled on the "and" of 4 for drive.
- Long stretches of the same groove; change comes from the guitars.
- Rapid snare rolls and tom flurries as transitions, sometimes a whole bar of 16ths.

## Fills
A bar of straight 16ths on snare crescendoing (velocity 60 → 120), or 16ths across toms.
End on crash + kick.

## Humanize
Even velocities (±4) with rushing-ahead energy. Never laid back.
