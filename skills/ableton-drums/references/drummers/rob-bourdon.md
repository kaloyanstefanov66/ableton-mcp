# Rob Bourdon (Linkin Park)

Read with `genres/alt-metal.md`.

**Feel:** precise and almost programmed. Hip-hop–influenced kick patterns, simple backbeats,
huge choruses. He serves the song with very few flourishes.
**Tempo range:** 95–110 BPM typical (nu-metal era).

## Vocabulary
- Verses: tight 8th/16th hats with syncopated hip-hop kicks.
- Choruses: crash on every quarter, kick doubling the guitar's chugs, snare on 2 and 4 at full force.
- Half-time bridges with china.
- Fills: short 16th snare/tom runs at section ends, often only one beat long.
- Stops and "drops": everything cuts out for a beat before a chorus.

## Fills
One-beat 16th fills into choruses, ending with crash + china + kick on the 1. Pre-chorus stop
(silence on beat 4 of the last bar).

## Humanize
Minimal: velocity ±3, timing ±3 ms. Snare 125, kick 115–120, crashes 110–118.
