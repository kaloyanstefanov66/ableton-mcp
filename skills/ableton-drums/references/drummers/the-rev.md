# The Rev (Avenged Sevenfold)

Read with `genres/metalcore.md` (early era) or `genres/thrash.md` (later era).

**Feel:** virtuosic and theatrical. Very fast double-kick, punk/skank beats, blast bursts, and
constant variation: he rarely repeats a 4-bar phrase exactly. Swing and shuffle feels show up too.
**Tempo range:** 110–230 BPM.

## Vocabulary
- 16th double-kick under choruses with crash or china on quarters.
- Punk beats (kick-snare alternating 8ths) at high tempo.
- Blast-beat bursts (1–2 bars) between sections.
- Busy linear fills across many toms, 16th sextuplets, crash chokes.
- Swing/shuffle sections with ride.

## Fills
Change the fill every time. Mix 16th triplets across toms, kick-snare-tom linear runs, and
two-beat blasts. Land with crash + china + kick.

## Variation rule
When writing repeats, change one element every 2 bars: kick variation, a cymbal choice, or a mini fill.

## Humanize
Tight: velocity ±5, timing ±5 ms. Double-kick alternating 115/105.
