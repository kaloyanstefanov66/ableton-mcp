# Lars Ulrich (Metallica)

Read with `genres/thrash.md`.

**Feel:** riff-driven and punchy. He plays the riff's rhythm with the band (gallops, stops,
accents) more than he keeps abstract time. Fast eras use skank beats; the 90s eras are mid-tempo
and big.
**Tempo range:** 90–110 (mid-tempo heavy) and 170–215 (thrash).

## Vocabulary
- Gallop kicks under gallop riffs (8th + two 16ths).
- Crash on every beat or every 8th at climaxes, often with the open hat.
- Tom-heavy fills that land as accents with the band.
- Stop-time: the band hits, everything stops, he fills the gap.
- Double-kick 16ths under fast riff passages, usually 1–2 bars at a time.

## Fills
Fills follow the riff's accent pattern: snare-tom-tom-floor in 16ths, then crash with the band.
Triplet tom fills at mid tempos.

## Humanize
Loud and slightly pushing: velocity ±6, timing ±8 ms, snare and crashes 115–127.
