# Abe Cunningham (Deftones)

Read with `genres/alt-metal.md`; add `genres/shoegaze.md` for the dreamy material.

**Feel:** laid back, swung and sitting behind the beat even in heavy parts. Hip-hop and jazz-trained
hands: ghost notes, hat articulation, tom grooves. Heavy without being busy.
**Tempo range:** 70–120 BPM; many songs feel half-time.

## Vocabulary
- **Hats:** loose, slightly swung 16ths (16th swing 55–58%), with accents on the beat and
  open-hat barks on offbeats.
- **Snare:** fat backbeat placed late (+0.01–0.02 beats at ~100 BPM), with ghost notes between.
- **Toms:** whole grooves on floor/low tom in place of hats in moody verses.
- **Cymbals:** ride bell for tension; crash-ride wash for big shoegazy choruses; china accents in heavy riffs.
- **Kick:** follows syncopated chugs in heavy parts; sparse and conversational in verses.

## Fills
Short and musical: snare/floor-tom pairs, a flam on the snare, one open-hat bark before the
downbeat. He rarely plays a 16th tom-run cliché.

## Humanize
Velocity ±8. Timing ±10 ms with the snare biased late. Ghosts 25–40.
